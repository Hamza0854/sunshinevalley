Welcome to Sunshine Valley

A second home, full of sunshine

At Sunshine Valley Academy we believe the early years matter more than any other. It’s when children learn to trust, to share, to ask questions and to love learning.

Our classrooms are bright and colourful, our carers are patient and trained, and every day balances structured learning with plenty of play. Parents drop off with confidence — children run in happy.

  • Small groups by age, so no child gets lost
  • Early literacy and numeracy through play
  • Rest time in a calm, clean nap room
  • Children only released to people you authorise

A day at Sunshine Valley

Children thrive on rhythm. Every day follows a gentle routine that mixes learning, play, meals and rest.

  1. 7:00

    Warm welcome

    Drop-off, free play and breakfast for early arrivals.

  2. 9:00

    Morning circle

    Songs, greetings and the day’s theme.

  3. 9:30

    Learning time

    Letters, numbers and hands-on activities in small groups.

  4. 10:30

    Snack & outdoor play

    A healthy snack, then out to the play area.

  5. 12:00

    Lunch

    A proper, balanced meal together.

  6. 1:00

    Nap & quiet time

    Rest in the calm nap room.

  7. 3:00

    Creative corner

    Art, stories, music and free play.

  8. 5:00

    Home time

    Hand-over to parents with a note on the day.
